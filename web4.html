Web 4.0

Web 4.0 represents the next frontier: a fully intelligent, ubiquitous web that seamlessly integrates physical and digital worlds. It envisions ultra-intelligent agents, IoT integration, and human-machine symbiosis.

Characteristics of Web 4.0

  • Integration of artificial intelligence and machine learning
  • Decentralized technologies and blockchain applications.
  • Enhanced user experience and personalization.
  • Improved data interoperability and sharing.
  • Increased focus on user privacy and security.
